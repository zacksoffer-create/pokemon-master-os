    How to play

    One finger does everything. Whoever has the ball is you; when you pass it, you're the man you passed to.

    ✍️Drag to dribble. Push the way you want to go; push further to run harder. He keeps the ball close until you let go. 👆Flick to pass. Flick toward a teammate and it finds him, leading him if he's running. A harder flick is a firmer pass; a long one goes in the air. 🥅Flick at the goal to shoot. Pick a corner. Harder is higher: a full-power flick can fly over. Bow the flick to bend it. ✋Hold your finger still to look up. Everything slows down, and your forwards make their runs in behind. You get a few seconds of it each attack. ⚡Tap to fake a pass. A defender diving in can bite. Flick while a pass is on its way and he plays it first time. 🧠The yellow arrows are runs. The blue line is offside: play it before your man is past it. Go past defenders at an angle, not straight at them, and cut as they come in.
