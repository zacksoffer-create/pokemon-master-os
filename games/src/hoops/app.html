Basketball

Playground

Flick to shoot. Read the defender, then make the play.

Moves
Flick up
Shoot. Flick harder for more distance.
Long swipe
Drive left or right.
Short swipe
Jab. Jumpy defenders shift their weight; go the other way.
Snap back
Swipe the other way mid-drive to cross him over.
Swipe down
Step back. Flick as you land for a step-back jumper; slow-footed defenders get left behind.
Diagonal down
Side-step. Hop sideways, away from the way he's leaning, then shoot.
Flick mid-drive
Pull-up. Stop on a dime and rise from where you are.
Dip, then flick
Fadeaway. Pull the ball down a little before you flick up. You drift back, his contest counts for less, and the shot is a touch harder.
Tap
Pump fake.
Euro step
After a blow-by, swipe sideways on the way to the rim.
Spin
Curl your layup flick. A little spin sits soft on the rim; too much spins it out.
Chain it
Moves you make while another is finishing go next.

HORSE

Make your shot and they have to match it from the same spot. Miss a match and you take a letter.
