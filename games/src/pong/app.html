Beer pong

PongNight

Flick to throw. Read the rack and the guy across the table, then pick your shot.

Throws
Flick up
Arc. A high lob that drops in steep. A harder flick goes deeper. Best for cups hiding behind other cups.
Short snap
Fastball. A quick, short flick throws it flat and hard into the back wall of the cup. Forgiving on depth, but it hits the first rim on its line, so save it for a naked front cup.
Dip, then flick
Bounce. Pull down a little, then flick up. It skips off the table and up into a cup. Counts for two, but some people swat it.
Curl
Spin. A little curl rides the rim and rolls in. Too much spins it out. Some house rules let them blow out a spinning ball.
Re-rack
At six cups and at three, you call the shape. Pick one that fits your best shot.
Balls back
Make both and you shoot two more.
On fire
Two in a row heats you up. Three and you're on fire: a bigger window, and you keep shooting until you miss.
Rebuttal
When your last cup goes, you shoot until you miss. Clear theirs and it's overtime.
