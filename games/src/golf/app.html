Golf

Fairway

Pull back, swing through. Read the wind, the lie and the flag, then pick the shot.

Your swing
Pull back
Touch anywhere and pull straight down. How far back is how far it goes. The gold notch on the arc is your number.
Swing through
Swing up past where you started. Come through at your own tempo for a pure strike; too quick pulls it left, easing off hits it fat.
Finish short
Knockdown. Stop your swing just past where you started. Low, under the wind, and it runs.
Finish long
High ball. Follow all the way up. Climbs, lands soft, rides a tailwind, and balloons into a headwind.
Bend it
Bow your swing and the ball bends the same way. Bow it right to draw it left; bow it left to fade it right.
Swipe sideways
Aim left or right before you swing.
Club arrows
One more club or one less. The game picks the club that carries the flag; the read is yours.
Putting
Same motion, smaller. Pull back for pace, push through. Lighter green is higher, and the drifting specks roll downhill.
