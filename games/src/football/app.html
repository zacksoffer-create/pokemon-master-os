    How the option works

    One play, three people who might end up with the ball. You make two reads in about two seconds, then you're the runner.

    ✋Hold GIVE to snap. Keep holding through the exchange and the fullback gets it. Let go and you pull it back. ①Watch the end. Crashing in on the dive? Pull it. Sitting square or coming at you? Give it. ②Pulled it? Watch the next man out. Coming for you? PITCH. Running with the pitch man? Keep it. 🕹Left thumb steers whoever has the ball. Let go and he runs upfield. ↕Right thumb: swipe up or down to juke. Tap to lower your shoulder and truck. Truck the small guys; juke the big ones.

    Read help puts a ring on the two defenders you're reading and slows the exchange. Turn it off in settings when you've got it.
