At Bat

Read the pitcher. Know the count. Put a swing on it.

The swing
Touch and hold
Load. Get your foot down early: a loaded swing has more pop.
Drag
Move your barrel (the gold ring) to where you want the ball. Set it before the pitch and sit on that spot, or chase it down.
Swipe through
Swing. Timing is when the fast part of your swipe starts: early pulls it, late pushes it the other way.
Swipe angle
Up and across lifts it. Flat lines it. Down chops it into the ground.
Swipe speed
Harder swings hit it farther but give you less time to be right.
Short, quick swipe
Contact swing. A bigger barrel and a quicker bat, less power. Your two-strike swing.
Don't swipe
Take. Balls are balls: the zone is the real one.
Tap
Re-center your barrel between pitches. Tap during a play to skip ahead.
The read
Every pitcher has a plan for every count. Learn his, and the situation tells you which swing to bring.
