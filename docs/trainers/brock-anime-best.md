**Tags:** Gym Leader · Multi-Region Traveler (Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh)

## Who they are

- Brock plays defense first and makes you come to them.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Gym Leader: a specialist's team built around one idea.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (14 of 19 attacks).

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fire: Forretress, Croagunk and Steelix are weak to it, and only Marshtomp can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Forretress to Fire; Marshtomp to Grass; Croagunk to Psychic (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Forretress and Steelix are specially soft.
- Watch the abilities: Croagunk's Dry Skin changes Fire and Water (from neutral to immune and weak).
- Don't lead with Steel or Dark: 4 of 6 resist Steel; 3 of 6 resist Dark.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Forretress's Self-Destruct (faints); Steelix's Iron Tail (75%) and Rock Tomb (80%).

## Career

- Brock has competed across 4 regions (Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh): expect answers built against a wide field.

## The ace

- The story's ace is Steelix; the data's is Croagunk (set up by Forretress, the widest coverage and a wallbreaker). Plan for both.

## Who they go to

- Even game: Steelix is the workhorse (tank).
- Behind: they turn to Croagunk (wallbreaker).
- Last resorts when it's slipping away: Forretress's Self-Destruct.

- Bring Flying: they can't hit it super-effectively, and two of them are weak to it.
