## Who they are

- Wallace sits in the middle: balanced between attack and defense, a plan with some give in it.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Region Champion: the last line of the league. Expect a team with no easy answers.
- Type-dominant: Water (6 of 6). Everything you know about that type applies to most of their team.
- Cohesive: Surf on Ludicolo, Whiscash and Gyarados. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the special side (14 of 17 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Tentacruel, Gyarados and Milotic outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Wailord, Tentacruel, Gyarados and Milotic are weak to it, and only Whiscash can take it.
- Also open to Grass (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Whiscash to Grass; Gyarados to Electric (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Tentacruel (physically soft).
- Don't lead with Steel, Fire or Water: 6 of 6 resist Steel; 5 of 6 resist Fire; 5 of 6 resist Water.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Wailord's Double-Edge (recoil) and Blizzard (70%); Tentacruel's Hydro Pump (80%); Milotic's Hydro Pump (80%).
