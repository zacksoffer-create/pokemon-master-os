## Who they are

- Paul comes at you with force and runs it the same way every time: one plan, executed hard.
- Close to their Pokémon, and demanding of them: tough love, not coldness.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (10 physical, 11 special).
- Fast where it counts: Ninjask, Weavile and Electabuzz outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called rigid (85), but this team reads flexible (50): low move-type variety: Ninjask, Ursaring and Electabuzz.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fire: Ninjask, Weavile and Torterra are weak to it, and only Gastrodon can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Ninjask to Rock; Weavile to Fighting; Gastrodon to Grass; Torterra to Ice (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Weavile and Electabuzz are physically soft.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Rigid, and it shows: Ninjask, Ursaring and Electabuzz can't hit back outside their attack types. Once you've seen the plan, it won't change.
- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Ursaring's Focus Blast (70%); Electabuzz's Thunder (70%); Torterra's Stone Edge (80%).
