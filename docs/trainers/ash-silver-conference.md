## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (13 physical, 9 special).
- Fast where it counts: Pikachu, Charizard and Heracross outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called gambler (70), but this team reads percentage player (33): carries a gamble: Pikachu and Totodile.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Charizard, Totodile and Kingler are weak to it, and only Pikachu can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock; Heracross to Flying (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Kingler (specially soft).
- Watch the abilities: Snorlax's Thick Fat changes Fire and Ice (from neutral to resists).
- Don't lead with Steel or Ice: 4 of 6 resist Steel; 3 of 6 resist Ice.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Pikachu's Thunder (70%) and Iron Tail (75%); Totodile's Hydro Pump (80%). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
