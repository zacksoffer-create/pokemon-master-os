## Who they are

- Lance plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- A Region Champion: the last line of the league. Expect a team with no easy answers.
- Type-dominant: Flying (5 of 6). Everything you know about that type applies to most of their team.
- Cohesive: Hyper Beam on Gyarados, Dragonair, Dragonite and Dragonite. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (14 physical, 8 special).
- Fast where it counts: Gyarados, Aerodactyl, Charizard, Dragonite and Dragonite outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called rigid (55), but this team reads flexible (17): low move-type variety: Dragonair.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Rock: Gyarados, Aerodactyl, Charizard, Dragonite and Dragonite are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Ice (4 weak), Electric (3 weak) and Dragon (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Gyarados to Electric; Charizard to Rock; Dragonite to Ice; Dragonite to Ice (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out as soft across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.
- Don't lead with Fire, Ground or Bug: 6 of 6 resist Fire; 5 of 6 resist Ground; 5 of 6 resist Bug.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Dragonair's Slam (75%); Dragonite's Dragon Rush (75%) and Thunder (70%).
