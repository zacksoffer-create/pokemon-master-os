## Who they are

- Giovanni comes at you with force and runs it the same way every time: one plan, executed hard.
- Their Pokémon are tools to them, used and replaced.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (8 of 11 attacks).
- Called rigid (70), but this team reads flexible (25): low move-type variety: Honchkrow.
- Called percentage player (40), but this team reads percentage player (0): no carries a gamble.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Nidoking, Honchkrow and Nidoqueen are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Water (2 weak).
- Their bulk is even across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Rigid, and it shows: Honchkrow can't hit back outside its attack types. Once you've seen the plan, it won't change.
