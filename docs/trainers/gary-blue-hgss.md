**Tags:** Gym Leader

## Who they are

- Gary plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- A Gym Leader: a specialist's team built around one idea.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (14 of 18 attacks).
- Called rigid (50), but this team reads flexible (16): no Choice items; low move-type variety: Exeggutor and Pidgeot.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Exeggutor, Rhydon and Pidgeot are weak to it, and only Arcanine can take it.
- Also open to Rock (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Exeggutor to Bug; Rhydon to Water and Grass; Gyarados to Electric (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Exeggutor and Rhydon are specially soft.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Arcanine's Flare Blitz (recoil); Rhydon's Stone Edge (80%); Machamp's Dynamic Punch (50%) and Stone Edge (80%).
