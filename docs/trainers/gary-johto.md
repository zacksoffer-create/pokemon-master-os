## Who they are

- Gary sits in the middle: balanced between attack and defense, a plan with some give in it.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (12 physical, 7 special).
- Called flexible (40), but this team reads rigid (83): low move-type variety: Arcanine, Nidoqueen, Magmar, Golem and Scizor.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Water: Arcanine, Nidoqueen, Magmar and Golem are weak to it, and only Blastoise can take it.
- Also open to Ground (4 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Golem to Water and Grass; Scizor to Fire (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Golem and Scizor are specially soft.
- Don't lead with Bug or Poison: 4 of 6 resist Bug; 3 of 6 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Blastoise's Hydro Pump (80%); Arcanine's Take Down (recoil) and Fire Spin (70%).
