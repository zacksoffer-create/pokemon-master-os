## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- Cohesive: Tackle on Tauros, Squirtle and Bulbasaur. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (13 physical, 9 special).
- Fast where it counts: Tauros, Charizard and Pikachu outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called flexible (35), but this team reads rigid (67): low move-type variety: Tauros, Squirtle, Bulbasaur and Pikachu.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Lapras, Squirtle and Charizard are weak to it, and only Bulbasaur and Pikachu can take it.
- Also open to Rock (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Tauros (specially soft).
- Watch the abilities: Lapras's Water Absorb changes Water (from resists to immune).
- Don't lead with Steel: 3 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Tauros's Take Down (recoil); Charizard's Fire Spin (70%); Pikachu's Thunder (70%). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
