## Who they are

- Zack comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.
- No sliders are set for this version yet, so this reads from the team's data alone.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (12 physical, 7 special).
- Fast where it counts: Pikachu, Dragonite, Infernape and Gengar outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ground: Pikachu, Infernape and Empoleon are weak to it, and only Dragonite and Gengar can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Dragonite to Ice; Scizor to Fire (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Scizor is specially soft.
- Watch the abilities: Gengar's Levitate changes Ground (from weak to immune).
- Don't lead with Bug, Steel or Grass: 5 of 6 resist Bug; 4 of 6 resist Steel; 4 of 6 resist Grass.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Pikachu's Volt Tackle (recoil) and Iron Tail (75%).
