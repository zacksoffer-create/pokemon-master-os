## Who they are

- Brandon plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- Cohesive: Focus Punch on Regice, Regirock and Registeel; Hyper Beam on Regice, Regirock and Registeel. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (6 physical, 11 special).
- Called offensive (70), but this team reads defensive (39): attack-heavy stats: Ninjask and Solrock; mostly attacks: Solrock, Regice, Regirock and Registeel; fast: Ninjask.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Steel: Solrock, Regice and Regirock are weak to it, and only Registeel can take it.
- Also open to Fighting (3 weak), Fire (3 weak) and Water (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Ninjask to Rock (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Solrock (specially soft).
- Watch the abilities: Solrock's Levitate changes Ground (from weak to immune).
- Don't lead with Normal or Poison: 4 of 6 resist Normal; 4 of 6 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Solrock's Psywave (80%); Regice's Zap Cannon (50%); Regirock's Stone Edge (80%).
