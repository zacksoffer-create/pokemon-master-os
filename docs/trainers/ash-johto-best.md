## Who they are

- Ash adapts mid-battle.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (13 of 19 attacks).
- Called flexible (30), but this team reads rigid (83): low move-type variety: Bayleef, Quilava, Totodile, Noctowl and Donphan.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Bayleef, Noctowl and Donphan are weak to it, and only Quilava and Totodile can take it.
- Also open to Flying (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Heracross to Flying (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Noctowl (physically soft) and Donphan (specially soft).

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Totodile's Hydro Pump (80%); Donphan's Take Down (recoil).
