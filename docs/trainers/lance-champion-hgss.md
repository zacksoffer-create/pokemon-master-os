## Who they are

- Lance plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- A Region Champion: the last line of the league. Expect a team with no easy answers.
- Type-dominant: Flying (6 of 6). Everything you know about that type applies to most of their team.
- Cohesive: Hyper Beam on Dragonite, Dragonite and Dragonite. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (13 physical, 8 special).
- Called rigid (60), but this team reads flexible (0): no Choice items; no low move-type variety.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Rock: Gyarados, Dragonite, Dragonite, Aerodactyl, Charizard and Dragonite are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Ice (4 weak), Electric (3 weak) and Dragon (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Gyarados to Electric; Dragonite to Ice; Dragonite to Ice; Charizard to Rock; Dragonite to Ice (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Gyarados is physically soft.
- Don't lead with Ground, Bug or Fire: 6 of 6 resist Ground; 6 of 6 resist Bug; 6 of 6 resist Fire.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Dragonite's Dragon Rush (75%) and Thunder (70%); Dragonite's Dragon Rush (75%) and Blizzard (70%).
