## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (13 physical, 9 special).
- Fast where it counts: Charizard, Pikachu and Sceptile outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called flexible (25), but this team reads rigid (67): low move-type variety: Bulbasaur, Squirtle, Sceptile and Torkoal.
- Called gambler (75), but this team reads percentage player (33): carries a gamble: Squirtle and Pikachu.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Rock: Charizard and Torkoal are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Sceptile (physically soft) and Torkoal (specially soft).
- Don't lead with Steel: 4 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Squirtle's Hydro Pump (80%); Pikachu's Volt Tackle (recoil) and Iron Tail (75%). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
