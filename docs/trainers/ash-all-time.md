## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (18 of 24 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Pikachu, Charizard, Sceptile, Infernape and Heracross outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called gambler (75), but this team reads percentage player (33): carries a gamble: Pikachu and Infernape.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Flying: Sceptile, Infernape and Heracross are weak to it, and only Pikachu can take it.
- Also open to Psychic (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock; Heracross to Flying (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Sceptile, Snorlax and Heracross are physically soft.
- Don't lead with Grass or Steel: 4 of 6 resist Grass; 3 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Pikachu's Volt Tackle (recoil) and Iron Tail (75%); Infernape's Flare Blitz (recoil). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.

## Career

- Ash has competed across 8 regions (Kanto, Orange Archipelago, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos and Alola): expect answers built against a wide field.
- Titles: Orange League Champion, Alola Champion and Monarch (World Champion).
- Recent record: Lumiose Conference: runner-up (lost to Alain); Manalo Conference: won (beat Gladion); World Coronation Series: won the Masters Eight (beat Leon).

## The ace

- Ace: Pikachu. The story and the data agree (the most invested and a revenge killer).

## Who they go to

- Even game: Snorlax is the workhorse (bulky attacker).
- Ahead: Sceptile closes it out (revenge killer).
- Behind: they turn to Heracross (wallbreaker).
