## Who they are

- Paul comes at you with force and runs it the same way every time: one plan, executed hard.
- Close to their Pokémon, and demanding of them: tough love, not coldness.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (15 of 21 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Electabuzz, Magmar and Nidoking outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ground: Electabuzz, Magmar, Nidoking and Lairon are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Water (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Lairon to Fighting and Ground (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Electabuzz and Magmar are physically soft.
- Don't lead with Bug: 4 of 6 resist Bug.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Rigid, and it shows: Hariyama, Electabuzz, Ursaring, Magmar and Lairon can't hit back outside their attack types. Once you've seen the plan, it won't change.
- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Electabuzz's Thunder (70%); Ursaring's Focus Blast (70%).
