## Who they are

- Misty sits in the middle: balanced between attack and defense, a plan with some give in it.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- A Gym Leader: a specialist's team built around one idea.
- Type-dominant: Water (6 of 6). Everything you know about that type applies to most of their team.
- Cohesive: Water Gun on Staryu, Psyduck, Politoed and Starmie. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (7 physical, 13 special).
- Fast where it counts: Staryu, Gyarados and Starmie outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Staryu, Psyduck, Corsola, Politoed, Gyarados and Starmie are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Grass (5 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Corsola to Grass; Gyarados to Electric (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out as soft across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.
- Watch the abilities: Politoed's Water Absorb changes Water (from resists to immune).
- Don't lead with Fire, Water or Steel: 6 of 6 resist Fire; 5 of 6 resist Water; 5 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Gyarados's Hydro Pump (80%); Starmie's Hydro Pump (80%).

## Career

- Misty has competed across 3 regions (Kanto, Orange Archipelago and Johto): expect answers built against a wide field.

## The ace

- The story's ace is Starmie; the data's is Corsola (set up by Starmie, the widest coverage and a sponge / status absorber). Plan for both.

## Who they go to

- Even game: Gyarados is the workhorse (pivot).
- Ahead: Starmie closes it out (late-game cleaner).
- Behind: they turn to Starmie (wallbreaker).

## What they can hit

- They hit 3 of 17 types super-effectively; nothing they carry hits Bug, Dark, Dragon, Electric, Fighting, Flying, Ghost, Grass, Ice, Normal, Poison, Psychic, Steel or Water that way.
- Bring Electric: they can't hit it super-effectively, and six of them are weak to it.
- Grass or Bug get through clean too.
