## Who they are

- Paul comes at you with force and runs it the same way every time: one plan, executed hard.
- Keeps their Pokémon at arm's length.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (13 of 18 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Staravia, Elekid and Murkrow outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Rock: Staravia, Murkrow and Chimchar are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Electric (3 weak) and Ice (3 weak).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Grotle (specially soft).
- Watch the abilities: Azumarill's Thick Fat changes Fire and Ice (from resists to resists).
- Don't lead with Steel: 3 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Rigid, and it shows: Staravia, Elekid, Grotle and Murkrow can't hit back outside their attack types. Once you've seen the plan, it won't change.
- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Elekid's Thunder (70%).
