## Who they are

- Cynthia comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Region Champion: the last line of the league. Expect a team with no easy answers.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (10 physical, 14 special).
- Fast where it counts: Lucario, Milotic, Togekiss and Garchomp outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Biggest single openings: Gastrodon to Grass; Garchomp to Ice (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out as soft across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.
- Don't lead with Poison or Steel: 4 of 6 resist Poison; 3 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Lucario's Bone Rush (80%); Milotic's Hydro Pump (80%); Garchomp's Dragon Rush (75%).
- Flexible and well covered: no single trick beats them. Win the matchups one at a time.
