- Zack comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- Cohesive: Curse on Feraligatr, Miltank, Donphan, Ampharos, Umbreon and Espeon; Dynamic Punch on Feraligatr, Miltank and Ampharos. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (9 physical, 8 special).

## How to beat them

- Attack with Bug: Umbreon and Espeon are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Grass (2 weak).
- Bring a special attacker: Miltank and Donphan are specially soft.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap
