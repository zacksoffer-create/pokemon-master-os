## Who they are

- Tyson plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (16 of 22 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Shiftry, Sceptile and Meowth outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fire: Shiftry, Metagross and Sceptile are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Flying (3 weak), Ice (3 weak) and Fighting (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Shiftry to Bug (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Sceptile (physically soft) and Donphan (specially soft).
- Don't lead with Electric or Rock: 3 of 6 resist Electric; 3 of 6 resist Rock.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Meowth's Iron Tail (75%) and Fury Swipes (80%).
