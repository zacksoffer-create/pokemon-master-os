## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Cohesive: Quick Attack on Sceptile, Swellow and Pikachu. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (18 of 23 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Heracross, Infernape, Sceptile, Swellow and Pikachu outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called flexible (20), but this team reads rigid (50): low move-type variety: Torterra, Sceptile and Swellow.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Flying: Heracross, Infernape, Torterra and Sceptile are weak to it, and only Pikachu can take it.
- Also open to Ice (3 weak), Fire (3 weak) and Psychic (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Heracross to Flying; Torterra to Ice (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Sceptile (physically soft).
- Don't lead with Grass: 4 of 6 resist Grass.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Infernape's Flare Blitz (recoil); Swellow's Brave Bird (recoil); Pikachu's Iron Tail (75%) and Volt Tackle (recoil). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
