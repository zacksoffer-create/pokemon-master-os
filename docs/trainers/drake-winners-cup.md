## Who they are

- Drake plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Gym Leader: a specialist's team built around one idea.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (10 physical, 6 special).
- Fast where it counts: Gengar, Venusaur, Electabuzz and Dragonite outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called rigid (50), but this team reads rigid (80): low move-type variety: Gengar, Venusaur, Electabuzz and Dragonite.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Onix, Venusaur and Dragonite are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Psychic (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Onix to Water and Grass; Dragonite to Ice (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Electabuzz (physically soft) and Onix (specially soft).
- Watch the abilities: Gengar's Levitate changes Ground (from weak to immune).
- Don't lead with Electric: 3 of 6 resist Electric.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Onix's Bind (75%); Dragonite's Thunder (70%) and Slam (75%).
