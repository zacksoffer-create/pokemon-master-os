**Tags:** Cohesive (Rock Slide) · Type-dominant (Rock) · Gym Leader

## Who they are

- Brock takes the first hit and grinds you down with a system that doesn't change.
- Close to their Pokémon, and demanding of them: tough love, not coldness.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Gym Leader: a specialist's team built around one idea.
- Type-dominant: Rock (5 of 5). Everything you know about that type applies to most of their team.
- Cohesive: Rock Slide on Graveler, Onix and Kabutops. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (10 of 13 attacks).
- Called rigid (70), but this team reads flexible (30): no Choice items; low move-type variety: Graveler, Rhyhorn and Onix.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Grass: Graveler, Rhyhorn, Omastar, Onix and Kabutops are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Fighting (5 weak), Ground (5 weak) and Water (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Graveler to Water and Grass; Rhyhorn to Water and Grass; Omastar to Grass; Onix to Water and Grass; Kabutops to Grass (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Graveler, Rhyhorn, Omastar, Onix and Kabutops are specially soft.
- Don't lead with Normal, Flying or Poison: 5 of 5 resist Normal; 5 of 5 resist Flying; 5 of 5 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Rigid, and it shows: Graveler, Rhyhorn and Onix can't hit back outside their attack types. Once you've seen the plan, it won't change.
- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Rhyhorn's Horn Drill (one-hit KO, 30%); Onix's Iron Tail (75%).
