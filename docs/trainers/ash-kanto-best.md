## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (14 physical, 8 special).
- Fast where it counts: Pikachu, Charizard and Tauros outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called flexible (30), but this team reads rigid (67): low move-type variety: Bulbasaur, Squirtle, Kingler and Tauros.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Charizard, Squirtle and Kingler are weak to it, and only Pikachu and Bulbasaur can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Kingler and Tauros are specially soft.
- Don't lead with Steel: 4 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Pikachu's Volt Tackle (recoil) and Iron Tail (75%); Squirtle's Hydro Pump (80%); Tauros's Take Down (recoil). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
