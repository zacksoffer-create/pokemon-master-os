## Who they are

- Cynthia comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Region Champion: the last line of the league. Expect a team with no easy answers.
- They attack mostly from the special side (18 of 23 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Roserade, Lucario and Garchomp outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Roserade, Togekiss and Garchomp are weak to it, and only Lucario and Milotic can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Garchomp to Ice (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Roserade (physically soft).
- Don't lead with Poison: 3 of 6 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Lucario's Stone Edge (80%); Garchomp's Dragon Rush (75%).
