## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Cohesive: Quick Attack on Pikachu, Grovyle and Swellow. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (13 physical, 7 special).
- Fast where it counts: Pikachu, Grovyle, Swellow and Glalie outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called flexible (35), but this team reads rigid (67): low move-type variety: Grovyle, Swellow, Corphish and Torkoal.
- Called gambler (70), but this team reads percentage player (17): carries a gamble: Pikachu.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Rock: Swellow, Torkoal and Glalie are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Torkoal (specially soft).

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Pikachu's Thunder (70%) and Iron Tail (75%). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
