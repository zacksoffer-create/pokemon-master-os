## Who they are

- Barry plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (15 physical, 8 special).
- Fast where it counts: Staraptor, Heracross and Roserade outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called gambler (75), but this team reads percentage player (33): carries a gamble: Staraptor and Empoleon.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fire: Heracross, Roserade and Skarmory are weak to it, and only Snorlax can take it.
- Also open to Electric (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Heracross to Flying (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Staraptor and Skarmory are specially soft.
- Watch the abilities: Snorlax's Thick Fat changes Fire and Ice (from neutral to resists).
- Don't lead with Ghost, Bug or Grass: 4 of 6 resist Ghost; 4 of 6 resist Bug; 4 of 6 resist Grass.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Staraptor's Brave Bird (recoil); Empoleon's Hydro Pump (80%). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
