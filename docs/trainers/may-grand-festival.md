## Who they are

- May adapts mid-battle.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- Cohesive: Tackle on Venusaur, Munchlax and Skitty. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- Their attacks split between physical and special (10 physical, 10 special).
- Called flexible (30), but this team reads rigid (67): low move-type variety: Blaziken, Venusaur, Beautifly and Skitty.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Flying: Blaziken, Venusaur and Beautifly are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Psychic (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Beautifly to Rock (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Munchlax (physically soft).

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Blaziken's Fire Spin (70%); Skitty's Blizzard (70%).
