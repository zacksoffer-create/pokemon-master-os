## Who they are

- Gary adapts mid-battle.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (13 physical, 7 special).
- Called flexible (30), but this team reads rigid (83): low move-type variety: Umbreon, Scizor, Nidoqueen, Arcanine and Electivire.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ground: Nidoqueen, Arcanine and Electivire are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Biggest single openings: Scizor to Fire (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out as soft across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.
- Watch the abilities: Electivire's Motor Drive changes Electric (from resists to immune).
- Don't lead with Steel: 4 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Arcanine's Take Down (recoil) and Fire Spin (70%); Blastoise's Hydro Pump (80%); Electivire's Thunder (70%).
