## Who they are

- Paul comes at you with force and runs it the same way every time: one plan, executed hard.
- Close to their Pokémon, and demanding of them: tough love, not coldness.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (11 physical, 8 special).
- Fast where it counts: Electivire, Ninjask, Froslass and Drapion outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called rigid (85), but this team reads flexible (33): low move-type variety: Ninjask and Froslass.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fire: Torterra, Ninjask and Froslass are weak to it, and only Gastrodon can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Torterra to Ice; Ninjask to Rock; Gastrodon to Grass (four times the damage).
- Their bulk is even across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.
- Watch the abilities: Electivire's Motor Drive changes Electric (from resists to immune).
- Don't lead with Poison: 3 of 6 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Rigid, and it shows: Ninjask and Froslass can't hit back outside their attack types. Once you've seen the plan, it won't change.
- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Torterra's Stone Edge (80%); Froslass's Blizzard (70%).

## Career

- Recent record: Battle Frontier: challenged the Battle Pyramid and lost to Brandon (draft: confirm); Lily of the Valley Conference: Top 8 (lost to Ash in a full battle).

## The ace

- The story's ace is Electivire; the data's is Torterra (set up by Ninjask, the widest coverage and a bulky attacker). Plan for both.

## Who they go to

- Even game: Gastrodon is the workhorse (tank).
- Ahead: Froslass closes it out (revenge killer).
- Behind: they turn to Torterra (wallbreaker).
