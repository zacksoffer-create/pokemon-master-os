## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (16 of 23 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Staraptor, Buizel, Gliscor and Infernape outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.
- Called gambler (70), but this team reads percentage player (33): carries a gamble: Staraptor and Infernape.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Staraptor, Torterra, Gliscor and Gible are weak to it, and only Buizel and Infernape can take it.
- Also open to Flying (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Torterra to Ice; Gliscor to Ice; Gible to Ice (four times the damage).
- No side of their bulk stands out across the team, so pick attackers by type. The one soft spot: Staraptor (specially soft).

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Staraptor's Brave Bird (recoil); Infernape's Flare Blitz (recoil). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
