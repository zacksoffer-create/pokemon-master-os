## Who they are

- Misty plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- A Gym Leader: a specialist's team built around one idea.
- Type-dominant: Water (4 of 4). Everything you know about that type applies to most of their team.
- Cohesive: Water Pulse on Golduck, Quagsire, Lapras and Starmie. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the special side (7 of 9 attacks).
- Called gambler (50), but this team reads percentage player (0): no carries a gamble.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Grass: Golduck, Quagsire, Lapras and Starmie are weak to it, and nobody on the team resists it.
- Also open to Electric (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Quagsire to Grass (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Quagsire is specially soft.
- Watch the abilities: Lapras's Water Absorb changes Water (from resists to immune).
- Don't lead with Water, Steel or Fire: 3 of 4 resist Water; 3 of 4 resist Steel; 3 of 4 resist Fire.
