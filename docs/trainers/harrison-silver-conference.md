## Who they are

- Harrison plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.

## What to expect

- Cohesive: Faint Attack on Kecleon, Houndoom and Sneasel. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (15 of 22 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Houndoom, Sneasel and Blaziken outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fighting: Kecleon, Steelix, Houndoom and Sneasel are weak to it, and only Hypno can take it.
- Also open to Ground (3 weak) and Water (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Sneasel to Fighting (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Kecleon and Houndoom are physically soft.
- Don't lead with Ice: 3 of 6 resist Ice.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Kecleon's Fury Swipes (80%); Steelix's Iron Tail (75%) and Bind (75%); Houndoom's Smog (70%); Blaziken's Fire Spin (70%).
