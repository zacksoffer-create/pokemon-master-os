## Who they are

- Ash comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- They attack mostly from the physical side (17 of 24 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Pikachu, Infernape, Staraptor, Gliscor and Buizel outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Ice: Torterra, Staraptor and Gliscor are weak to it, and only Infernape and Buizel can take it.
- Biggest single openings: Torterra to Ice; Gliscor to Ice (four times the damage).
- Bring a special attacker: Staraptor and Gliscor are specially soft.
- Don't lead with Steel: 3 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Pikachu's Iron Tail (75%) and Volt Tackle (recoil); Infernape's Flare Blitz (recoil); Staraptor's Brave Bird (recoil). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
