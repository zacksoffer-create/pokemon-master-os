## Who they are

- Ash plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A gambler: they'll reach for the long shot.

## What to expect

- Their attacks split between physical and special (11 physical, 10 special).
- Called flexible (40), but this team reads rigid (83): low move-type variety: Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Squirtle, Pidgeotto and Kingler.
- Called gambler (85), but this team reads percentage player (33): carries a gamble: Pikachu and Charizard.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Charizard, Squirtle, Pidgeotto and Kingler are weak to it, and only Pikachu and Bulbasaur can take it.
- Also open to Rock (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock (four times the damage).
- Their bulk is even across the team: pick attackers by type, not by side.
- Don't lead with Steel: 4 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Reckless, and it shows: Pikachu's Thunder (70%); Charizard's Fire Spin (70%). Their misses and recoil are your free turns.
