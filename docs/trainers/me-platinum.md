## Who they are

- Zack comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.
- No sliders are set for this version yet, so this reads from the team's data alone.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (14 of 20 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Infernape, Weavile, Roserade, Lucario and Garchomp outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fire: Weavile, Roserade, Lucario and Magnezone are weak to it, and only Infernape and Garchomp can take it.
- Also open to Ground (3 weak) and Fighting (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Weavile to Fighting; Garchomp to Ice; Magnezone to Ground (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Weavile and Roserade are physically soft.
- Don't lead with Grass, Dark or Poison: 4 of 6 resist Grass; 4 of 6 resist Dark; 3 of 6 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- No gambles to wait out: they won't beat themselves, so you have to beat them.
