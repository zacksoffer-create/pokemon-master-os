**Tags:** Cohesive (Blizzard) · Champion

## Who they are

- Red plays offense first.
- Their Pokémon fight as partners, not tools.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- Cohesive: Blizzard on Lapras, Snorlax and Blastoise. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the special side (16 of 23 attacks).
- Called rigid (55), but this team reads flexible (8): no Choice items; low move-type variety: Venusaur.
- Called percentage player (30), but this team reads gambler (83): carries a gamble: Pikachu, Lapras, Snorlax, Charizard and Blastoise.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Electric: Lapras, Charizard and Blastoise are weak to it, and only Pikachu and Venusaur can take it.
- Also open to Rock (2 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Charizard to Rock (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Pikachu and Snorlax are physically soft.
- Watch the abilities: Lapras's Water Absorb changes Water (from resists to immune).
- Don't lead with Steel: 3 of 6 resist Steel.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- Even playing the percentages, they carry gambles: Pikachu's Volt Tackle (recoil) and Iron Tail (75%); Lapras's Blizzard (70%); Snorlax's Blizzard (70%); Charizard's Flare Blitz (recoil); Blastoise's Focus Blast (70%) and Blizzard (70%).
