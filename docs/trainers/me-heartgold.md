## Who they are

- Zack comes at you with force and changes the plan on the fly: aggressive, and hard to read.
- A percentage player: they take the safe, sure option.
- No sliders are set for this version yet, so this reads from the team's data alone.

## What to expect

- A Champion: they've won before and play like it.
- Cohesive: Earthquake on Typhlosion, Tyranitar and Mamoswine. They share their tools, so one answer covers several of them.
- They attack mostly from the physical side (13 of 18 attacks).
- Fast where it counts: Typhlosion, Mamoswine and Crobat outspeed the typical Pokémon of the generation.

## How to beat them

- Attack with Fighting: Tyranitar, Mamoswine, Lapras and Snorlax are weak to it, and only Crobat can take it.
- Also open to Rock (3 weak), Water (3 weak) and Grass (3 weak).
- Biggest single openings: Tyranitar to Fighting (four times the damage).
- Bring a physical attacker: Lapras and Snorlax are physically soft.
- Watch the abilities: Lapras's Water Absorb changes Water (from resists to immune); Snorlax's Thick Fat changes Fire and Ice (from neutral to resists).
- Don't lead with Poison: 3 of 6 resist Poison.

## Where their rigidity or recklessness leaves a gap

- No gambles to wait out: they won't beat themselves, so you have to beat them.
