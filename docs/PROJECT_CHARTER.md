# Project Charter

*Restated in my own words from the master handoff (2026-10-19). Where I interpret rather than restate, I say so.*

## What this is

Six chapters of one life, each answering the question **"what does mastery mean?"** differently. These aren't six attempts at the same strongest team. The teams are companions first: they travel, camp, rescue, cook, carry, warn and live together. They also fight well, in a way that *shows* each chapter's kind of mastery. A team should feel **inevitable**, not optimized.

**Order of reasoning:** identity → team philosophy → companion fit → life/world function → role complementarity → battle expression → moves → legality → implementation. Mechanics can't redeem a philosophically wrong choice, and philosophy can't excuse nonsense.

## The six chapters

| Chapter | Thesis | Kind of mastery | Don't turn it into… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crystal | first bonds, care before competence | **attachment** | a retroactively polished team |
| FireRed | heroic clarity, ambition | **confidence** | raw damage |
| Emerald | *"We learned how to live out here."* | **adaptation**: making a viable life anywhere | another battlefield |
| Platinum | *"Every turn you survive is a mistake."* | **initiative**: every turn converted | generic balance or pointless coverage |
| SoulSilver | *"We've been here before." / "Show us anything. We have an answer."* | **experience**: the living playbook (Brady) | a cold control machine, or a prototype for HeartGold |
| HeartGold | *"Nothing you do matters."* (secure, not cruel) | **completeness**: permanence, home (Rivera) | win-rate optimization, or reopening the six |

No chapter is a failed draft of a later one. SoulSilver and HeartGold are both final forms: one has an answer for everything, the other needs no new answers.

## Authority rules

**LOCKED > WORKING > PROVISIONAL > ARCHIVE.** Evidence priority: later direct user correction or lock > earlier user lock > confirmed decisions > assistant summaries > spreadsheet snapshots > assistant speculation. An old spreadsheet never overrides a later conversation. An assistant suggestion never becomes canon without Zack. A locked *roster* doesn't mean every *move* is locked.

## Field-function policy (handoff §12)

1. Each team collectively covers its game's field functions. 2. **Surf and Fly are universal minimums**, normally as permanent moves. 3. Other field tasks may be covered by world ability, temporary moves or equipment, labeled as such. 4. No HM slaves. 5. Keep the *cartridge requirement* and the *world function* separate. 6. Be version-specific (Crystal, FireRed and Emerald have Flash; Emerald has Dive; Platinum has Defog; HGSS has Whirlpool and Rock Climb).

> ⚠️ **Open collision (verified 2026-10-19):** no member of the **Crystal** six or the **Platinum** six can learn Fly on cartridge, and neither six has a Waterfall learner. Rule 2 says it applies "going forward to every team." Either it's forward-looking (SoulSilver and later detailed work), or it collides with two established rosters. See `QUESTIONS_AND_EXPERIMENTS.md` Q1. Not resolved here.

## World rules

- Species ≠ individual. A repeated species is a *different* companion unless Zack decides it's the same one. There's no ban on repeats.
- Temperaments, biographies and relationships Claude proposes are **authoring candidates** until Zack validates them.
- Label the context whenever moving between lore and cartridge.
- Breeding is mastery and inheritance, not an IV factory. An egg move has to earn its slot.

## Working agreement

- Collaborate in conversation. Compare foundational candidates **two or three at a time**, with real arguments for and against.
- Challenge weak premises; say when something is only an experiment; don't turn half-thoughts into rules (e.g., the "Scizor as supply carrier" half-thought stays a half-thought).
- Keep three kinds of claim apart: **mechanical fact** (sourced) · **design interpretation** (reasoned) · **user decision** (Zack's).
- Don't make Zack retell history. These docs exist so the next session starts where this one ended.
