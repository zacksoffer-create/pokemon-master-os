# Blackthorn HQ: the web app (2026-10-20, D-055)

*One place for the whole project: the estate and the Glasshouse, the breeding program and every lineage, the biological atlas and the research journal, the trainer scouting engine, the team reports, the build library and the Lab, your open calls and the record. It runs on the Canon DB Sheet (D-025) as an Apps Script web app that only you can open.*

> Luxury in presentation. Science in structure. Pokémon in identity. Life in every environment.

### The engines that run in the browser

The app can't run Python, so the engines you tinker with are ported to JavaScript and held to the Python originals by tests that rebuild the same outputs both ways and require them to match exactly:

| In the browser | Ported from | Held to Python by |
|---|---|---|
| `web/25_scout.html` | trainers/report.py, matrix.py, sliders.py, roles.py, playsheet.py, battle/phrases.py | every trainer version, 60 random edits of them (sliders, roles, members, moves, natures, EVs, items, the reading level) and six exposure reads: 5,172 checks, sentence for sentence, with Python's rounding and sort orders |
| `web/85_field.html` | wilderness/engine.py team_report, camp_life.py | every lineup, 48 random teams in all six games, and a preview of every pending world record: 55 teams |
| `web/26_specimen.html` | specimen.py | PIDs (a shiny search, and Python's own MT19937 generator for the rest), natures, genders, Hidden Power, characteristics, stats, the Showdown set and the Batch Editor script: 78 checks |
| `web/28_teamroles.html` | trainers/team_roles.py (the four team roles and the build name) | every journey companion plus a stock and a competitive set in the suite; all 962 builds with `node app/test_roles4_parity.mjs` |
| `web/20_core.html` | trainers/era.py stats | the Lab's stats and Hidden Power for six builds across Gens II–IV |
| `web/27_battle.html` | battle/engine.py, moves.py, knowledge.py, evaluate.py, trainer_policy.py, grid.py, scouting.py, playsheet.py, profile.py, phrases.py, scouting_report.py, with @smogon/calc 0.12.0 in the page | every lineup (and three edited ones: builds, coach settings, a swap), every section of the scouting file and the what-if league: 2,365 sections, number for number and sentence for sentence. Kept from Python: half-to-even rounding, exact means (`statistics.mean`), the random team draws (MT19937 and `random.sample`), every sort order, and the damage table's fetch-and-replay. The browser builds the whole file in about 30 seconds; Python takes about five minutes |

Their data comes from packs the engine writes: `app/scout_pack.py` (scout.json: config, roles, phrases, charts, every species and move as each generation had it, every trainer version with its team resolved, the ladder), `app/field_pack.py` (field.json: every species' tiered evidence per game). `app/battle_pack.py` (battle.json: each generation's battle tables, the move book, learnable pools, accuracy, Pokédex entries, natures, the cartridge opponents, the usage field and its cached profile baseline, every lineup and the phrase book; calc.json: Smogon's calculator, loaded into the page when a battle page first opens). `app/advance_pack.py` keeps the engine's last Python run for the Advance scouting index and the live check; the breeding engine's lineages are precomputed for every line (`app/breeder_pack.py`, a few seconds since the search moved to `breeding/stock.py`); `app/room_pack.py` writes the Engine Room and the inbox; `app/library_pack.py` (library.json: every trainer and version, evidence templates, lenses, collections, the AI flags' texts).

### The Lab

Species, generation (II–IV), level, nature, ability, item, IVs (DVs in Gen II), EVs (stat experience in Gen II) and four moves. It works out:
- real stats (the engine's formula, E-038; checked against `trainers/era.py` in the tests);
- Hidden Power's type and power, the characteristic (Gen IV), and in Gen II the gender and shininess the DVs give;
- coverage (what the moves hit super-effectively, what walls them) and type defenses;
- each move against what the line can learn in any game up to Gen IV, and whether the move exists yet in that generation. That check is loose on purpose (D-024, D-048); the engine's era check is the strict one;
- a Showdown paste. **Save to Specimens** writes it to the Sheet, and a saved specimen reopens exactly.

## Honesty rules the app keeps

- **Measurements need a source** (D-055, verbatim): *"Measurements should be backed by real entered or simulated data, never presented as verified live readings without a data source."* Environment numbers come only from the Readings tab with their source; with no readings an instrument says "no reading yet" (D-058: nothing is generated). Targets are labelled "proposed".
- **The numbers decide** (D-058). Every verdict the app shows comes from an engine reading the game's numbers: fights from the battle engine and Smogon's calculator, reports and roles from stats, typing and moves, breeding from the games' egg and learnset data, field readiness from moves and abilities. What isn't a mechanic is labelled as what it is: your decisions and proposals (canon), and logged readings. The matchup board plays every pair out; their side as built, yours at their top level.
- **The lab never edits canon.** Scouting Lab and Battle Lab variants and Field readiness swaps are experiments; a variant you save is a row in the Variants tab until you say which to keep.
- **The live battle file says whether it matches.** Advance scouting builds each lineup live and compares it with the engine's last Python run, and says which sections differ if any do; the Battle Lab's "How it's built" says what the engine models and points to what it leaves out (switching, hazards, doubles).
- **A move the game can't read is left out and said so** in the Scouting Lab (the Python engine would stop); edited members' moves get the app's quick learnability check, labelled as such.
- **Proposals aren't canon.** Anime teams show as proposed, cartridge teams as from the cartridge, your teams as canon. An answer in Your calls is recorded, and becomes a decision only when it's written into canon.
- **Locks hold.** The app has no way to edit a locked record; the Sheet's lock guard still guards HeartGold's six (D-006).

## How it's built

```
engine/app/export.py      the engines' outputs as six JSON files in engine/app/data:
                           hq (Blackthorn: party, estate, Glasshouse, program, lineage, the record),
                           atlas, builds (library, moves, natures, type chart, the ladder book),
                           scouting (every report), journal, learnsets
engine/app/web/*.html     the app in parts: 00 head and design system, 10 art (hero, crest, icons,
                           estate plate, Glasshouse plan), 20 core (data, router, stat math),
                           30 home, 40 estate + Glasshouse, 50 breeding, 60 atlas + journal + search,
                           25-27 the engines in JavaScript (report, specimen, battle), 70 scouting + teams,
                           72 advance scouting, 75 Scouting Lab, 77 Battle Lab, 80 builds + Lab, 85 field,
                           23 breeding stock engine, 88 breeding stock pages,
                           24 Trainer Library engine (benchmarks, lenses, compare), 89 Trainer Library pages,
                           90 calls + record, 92 inbox + Engine Room
engine/app/build.py       → engine/app/dist:
                           Blackthorn-HQ-app.zip       the parts + the data (goes to Drive)
                           blackthorn-apps-script.zip  Code.gs, WebApp.gs, Tree, Hatch, manifest, INSTALL.txt
                           Blackthorn-HQ-preview.html  the whole app in one file, with a stand-in for the Sheet
engine/appscript/WebApp.gs the launcher: finds the newest Blackthorn-HQ-app*.zip in Drive, unzips it once
                           (kept in the script cache), serves the page, and answers the page's calls
```

**Navigation.** Apps Script serves the page inside a sandboxed frame where following a link loads a blank page (Google's rule: links there must open in a new tab or replace the whole window). So the app never follows a link of its own: `20_core.html` catches every in-app click and changes the page in place, `google.script.history` keeps the address bar and the back button in step, and `google.script.url` reads the address you opened (so `…/exec#/battlelab` opens the Battle Lab). External links open in a new tab, and nothing uses the browser's own `prompt()`; notes are asked in the drawer. In the preview the address's `#hash` does the same job. `app/test_frame.mjs` holds this: it builds a frame the way Apps Script does and clicks through the app.

The page talks to the Sheet through four calls:

| Call | Does |
|---|---|
| `apiData(name)` | one of the data files from the zip |
| `apiTab(name)` | a tab's rows, keyed by its header row (the first row with three or more filled cells), dates as text |
| `apiAppend(tab, row)` | adds a row to Eggs, Egg Walks, Plantings, Harvests, Readings, Feedings, Journal, Timeline, Specimens, Variants, My Pokémon, My Builds, My Teams or the Studbook (nothing else), plus a Timeline line. Text is never written as a formula. My Pokémon / My Builds / My Teams are your records: every save is a new row and nothing rewrites one (docs/design/DATA_CONTRACT.md) |
| `apiUpload(name, mime, base64)` / `apiImage(id)` | saves a picture (made smaller in the browser) into the Drive folder Blackthorn HQ artwork, and returns one of those pictures as a data URL; nothing outside that folder is ever returned |
| `apiAnswer(id, text, status)` | writes "Your call" and "Status" in Your Calls; a call the tab doesn't have yet gets a row |

The app's own tabs (green, after Setup) have their header in row 1; the header names are what the app reads.

Permissions it asks for: this Sheet only (`spreadsheets.currentonly`), Drive (`drive`: to find the zip, and to make the one artwork folder and add your pictures to it; Apps Script's DriveApp accepts no narrower permission for creating files), and showing windows in the Sheet. Deployed as *Execute as: Me*, *Who has access: Only myself*.

## Install and update

`engine/appscript/INSTALL.txt` (also inside the apps-script zip): upload `Blackthorn-HQ-app.zip` to Drive without unzipping it; paste `WebApp.gs` (and the updated `Code.gs` and manifest) into the Sheet's Apps Script; Deploy → New deployment → Web app.

Your records live in the Sheet's My Pokémon, My Builds and My Teams tabs, and every page reads them live. For the engines (team roles, the packs) to read them too, a snapshot goes into `engine/store/personal.json`: read the three tabs (Claude does it with the Sheets connector) and run `python3 -I engine/store/personal.py --from rows.json`, then rebuild. Nothing is ever sent to the Sheet.

To update the app: `python3 -I app/export.py` (after canon changes; it runs every pack), `python3 -I app/build.py`, upload the new zip. The newest one is served within two minutes; the old one goes to History (Drive versioning rule).

## Testing

- `python3 -I -W ignore -m unittest discover -s tests` includes the `WebApp` tests: every page part is valid JavaScript; the browser's report engine, wilderness read, specimen kernel and battle engine (three lineups, about a minute) against Python (above); the launcher (`appscript/test_webapp.mjs`, with fake Sheets, Drive and cache over the real zip) serves the page, returns each data file exactly, reads and writes only what it should; and the Lab's stats and Hidden Power match `trainers/era.py`.
- The whole battle file and the league: `python3 -I app/battle_pack.py --parity /tmp/py.json && node app/test_battle_parity.mjs /tmp/py.json` (about five minutes, nearly all of it Python).
- `python3 -I breeding/stock.py --parity /tmp/st.json && node app/test_stock_parity.mjs /tmp/st.json` (also in the suite): the browser's breeding stock against Python on the breeding program, the proposed lines and a fixed sample of species and sets in every game. `node app/test_stock.mjs` (after a build; also in the suite): the stock pages answer the spec's four questions, save, version, record a hatch and compare.
- `node app/test_library.mjs` (after a build; also in the suite): the Trainer Library against the spec's criteria 1–4 and 7–10: Cynthia's actual team, her versions compared, Sinnoh Ash's research, a team as a benchmark, collections with the rule shown and the bar movable (the Ace collections give the Ace test's own rates; every stamp matches Python), your own group, an optimized version that leaves the original alone, and the links from the Studio, team lab, scouting reports, Battle Lab and standards.
- `node app/test_labs.mjs` (after a build; also in the suite): the four labs open, a move changed in the Pokémon lab re-reads live, the team lab answers every question with candidates, the breeding lab and PKHeX export render.
- `python3 -I engine/rebuild.py`: every generator in dependency order, then what moved (engine/data_manifest/last_rebuild.json). `--list` shows the steps, `--only <step>` runs one.
- `node app/test_frame.mjs` (after a build; also in the suite): the app inside an Apps Script-style frame, every menu item, links and clickable rows on every page, at desktop and phone width; the window must never leave the app.
- `python3 -I app/build.py --shots [page …]` screenshots pages from the preview with the pre-installed Chromium and prints any JavaScript errors.

## Design system

Palette tokens in `00_head.html`: charcoal `#15171C`, gold `#C9A96A`, ivory `#F3EDE2`, forest `#5E8F68`, ice `#9CCBDD`, ember `#DE7C42`; the seventeen Gen IV type colors stay as the games have them. Cormorant Garamond for headings, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for numbers and ids. Official artwork for portraits; each chapter's own era sprites for teams. Cards with gold hairlines, the ornament divider, ivory specimen plates. It lays out down to phone width (tested at 390 px).
