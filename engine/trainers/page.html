Trainer Scouting Reports

Pick a trainer and a version. The folder shows who they are (four sliders, set from canon and judgment, with the team's own reading beside them), what to expect, how to beat them, and where their rigidity or recklessness leaves an opening. The matrix reads their team against every attacking type in that game's chart. No damage numbers and no simulation: the same team always gets the same report.

Data: our PokeAPI snapshot rolled back to each game's generation (type chart, typing, stats, abilities, learnsets); cartridge teams from the pret disassemblies. Every sentence is a rule in engine/trainers/phrases.json, and every threshold lives in engine/trainers/config.json. Proposed values are drafts until approved.
