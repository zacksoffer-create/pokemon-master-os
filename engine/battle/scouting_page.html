Journey Scouting File

Every team from the journey, and any variation of it, read the way a front office reads a series. The advance report shows how to attack each of their Pokémon with each attack type you throw. The self-scout covers what your club is good at and what it's trying to do: its coach profile, as sliders against what real players ran, and its play sheet. The game plan puts the two together, situation by situation. There are no grades: each line is a phrase triggered by the numbers. Switch on Show the triggers to see which number fired it.

Sources: Smogon's damage calculator (@smogon/calc 0.12.0) for every damage range; Smogon's usage-stats sets (@smogon/sets 2.0.0, MIT) for the field; opponents' teams, moves, items, AI flags, bags and battle lines read from the games' own code (pret disassemblies of Crystal, FireRed, Emerald, Platinum, HeartGold/SoulSilver); species, moves and Pokédex text from PokeAPI. The phrase book lives at engine/battle/phrasebook.json ( rules) and can be edited. Decisions D-043 and D-046.
