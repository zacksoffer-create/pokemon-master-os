Journey Scouting File

Every team from the journey, graded the way a scout grades a player and planned the way a coordinator plans a game. Tools are on the 20–80 scale and era-adjusted. Each call sheet comes from exact damage against the game's real Elite Four and champions.

Sources: Smogon's damage calculator (@smogon/calc 0.12.0) for every damage range; opponents' teams, moves, levels, items and IVs read from the games' own code (pret disassemblies of Crystal, FireRed, Emerald, Platinum, HeartGold/SoulSilver); species, moves and Pokédex data from PokeAPI. Builds and rosters from the project canon (decision D-043). Grades rank a Pokémon against its own generation; the what-if league puts every team under Gen 4 rules at Lv 100.
